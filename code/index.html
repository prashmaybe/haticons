Modular Category Imports

You don't need the entire icon set! Load only what your app uses—such as commerce.min.css (1.5 KB) or directional.min.css (1.5 KB).

Includes TypeScript definitions (index.d.ts), icon querying utilities, raw SCSS partials, and production-minified bundles.
