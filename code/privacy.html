Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount. Haticons is a client-side open-source project with zero tracking.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Haticons does not collect, sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. We do not require accounts, sign-ups, email addresses, phone numbers, or credit card information to use Haticons, browse the icons, or download our ZIP packages.

2. Local Storage & Cookies

Haticons does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We utilize standard browser localStorage solely for saving your client-side UI preferences:

  • haticons_theme_preference: Saves your preferred theme setting (system, light, or dark) so the website remembers your visual preference upon return.

3. Third-Party CDNs & Infrastructure

When you load Haticons via public Content Delivery Networks (such as jsDelivr or Google Fonts for web typography), your browser directly contacts those third-party networks. Those services operate under their own independent privacy policies (e.g., jsDelivr Privacy Policy).

4. External Links & Sandboxes

Our documentation contains buttons to launch live interactive demos on external developer platforms such as CodePen. Launching an interactive pen transmits standard HTML/CSS code snippet data to CodePen under CodePen's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the PebbleBenders maintainers via GitHub at github.com/prashmaybe/haticons.
